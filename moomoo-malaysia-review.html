Is Moomoo Malaysia regulated and safe?

Moomoo Securities Malaysia Sdn Bhd is licensed by the Securities Commission Malaysia (SC) under a Capital Markets Services Licence (No. eCMSL/A0397/2024), which permits it to operate as a legal brokerage in Malaysia.

Client funds are kept separate from Moomoo's own finances through a custodian bank arrangement — Moomoo does not have direct access to client cash or holdings. In addition, eligible Malaysia securities are covered under the Capital Market Compensation Fund (CMC Fund), which protects investors up to RM100,000 in the unlikely event the broker becomes unable to meet its obligations.

In practical terms: the regulatory and custody structure is comparable to any SC-licensed broker in Malaysia. The differentiator between brokers isn't safety, it's fees, features, and market access.

For pure Bursa-only, buy-and-hold investors, Moomoo's RM3/order platform fee is reasonable but not the cheapest — Rakuten Trade's flat, capped fee structure is generally lower for smaller Bursa trades. Moomoo's value proposition is strongest for investors who trade across multiple markets from one account.

What's good, and what needs work

✓ Where Moomoo stands out

  • Widest market access of any Malaysia-regulated broker in one account (MY, US, SG, HK, China)
  • Only platform with a built-in AI research tool for Malaysian retail investors
  • Free real-time Level 2 data most competitors charge for
  • Strong order-execution options for both beginners and active traders

⚠ Where it falls short

  • The in-app Help Centre is thinner than it should be for common questions — corporate actions, CDS account type, and AGM participation aren't always clearly documented
  • Customer support response times are commonly reported as inconsistent, particularly for account-specific queries outside standard FAQs
  • New users coming from Moomoo Singapore accounts report some friction being routed between regional support teams
  • Feature density can feel overwhelming for first-time investors compared to a simpler, Bursa-only platform

Verdict

Moomoo Malaysia earns its position as one of the most feature-complete brokers available locally — the combination of multi-market access, built-in AI tools, and free Level 2 data is hard to match at this price point. It isn't the cheapest option for investors who trade Bursa exclusively and rarely touch other markets, and its support infrastructure hasn't fully caught up with its feature set. For investors who want one account across Malaysia, US, and Asian markets, it remains a strong pick.
