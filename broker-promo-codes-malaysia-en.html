Promo code FAQ

What is the Moomoo promo code in Malaysia?

BURSA88. Key it in on Moomoo's Special Deposit screen when you make your first deposit. On top of Moomoo's standard new-user rewards (up to RM1,500 until 31 Dec 2026), BURSA88 adds Apple shares worth RM50 at a RM1,000 deposit or RM100 at RM10,000.

Where do I enter the Moomoo deposit code BURSA88?

In the Moomoo app, search for "Special Deposit", key in BURSA88, then make your deposit from that screen. The code has to be entered before the deposit is confirmed.

Does Webull Malaysia have a referral code?

Webull Malaysia does not ask you to type a code. The reward is tied to the referral link you sign up from. The BursaNews link is webull.com.my/k/BURSA88. Webull's current new-user offer is RM300 in NVIDIA shares when you keep a net deposit of RM5,000 for 60 days (until 31 Dec 2026).

What is the Rakuten Trade referral code?

BURSA88. Sign up through the BursaNews link, or go to rakutentrade.my, choose Educator under "How did you hear about us?" and enter BURSA88. When we last checked, Rakuten Trade listed RM0 brokerage on a new trader's first Bursa trade.

Which Malaysian broker gives the biggest sign-up reward?

In ringgit, Moomoo, at up to RM1,500 for a RM40,000 deposit. Per ringgit deposited, Moomoo's RM1,000 tier with BURSA88 is the richest at about 15% (RM150 in coupons and Apple shares). Webull pays 6% (RM300 on RM5,000). Rewards are coupons or shares, not cash, and need a 60-day hold.

What are the Moomoo rewards in Malaysia right now?

Moomoo's new user sign-up bonus is tiered by deposit until 31 Dec 2026: RM100 in coupons at RM1,000, rising to RM1,500 in coupons and Apple shares at RM40,000, with a 60-day hold. Code BURSA88 on the Special Deposit screen adds RM50 to RM100 in Apple shares on top.

Can I use more than one broker promo code?

Yes, if you open accounts with different brokers. Each broker gives its new-user reward once per person, so you cannot claim the same broker's reward twice.
