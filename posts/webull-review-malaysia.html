What Is Webull Malaysia?

Webull was founded in the US in 2016 and has since grown into a global brokerage with tens of millions of users across 14+ regulated markets. In Malaysia, it operates as Webull Securities (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd, offering access to Malaysian stocks, US stocks and options, Hong Kong shares, and China A-shares — the latter being a genuinely uncommon offering among Malaysia-regulated brokers, since direct A-share access for foreign investors has historically been restricted.

Is It Regulated and Safe?

Yes. Webull Securities (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd holds a Capital Markets Services Licence (CMSL) issued by the Securities Commission Malaysia under the Capital Markets and Services Act 2007, and is registered as a Participating Organisation of Bursa Malaysia. As with any SC-licensed broker, client assets are covered by the Capital Market Compensation Fund for broker-related default, up to RM100,000 per investor. This is not insurance against your investments losing value — no legitimate broker or regulator anywhere offers that.

Fees and Commission Structure

For Malaysian stocks, Webull charges zero commission and zero platform fee for a promotional period (check the in-app pricing page for the current end date, as this has been extended more than once). For US stocks, Webull is running 0% commission until 31 December 2026 — one of the more generous ongoing US-market promotions available from any Malaysia-regulated broker at the time of writing. After promotional periods end, standard low commission rates apply, with no platform fee, no account opening fee, and no inactivity fee.

💡 Webull also pays interest on unused cash sitting in your account — a detail that's easy to miss but adds up if you keep a cash buffer between trades. Check the current rate in-app, since it moves with market conditions.

Key Features Worth Knowing

Fractional Shares and 24-Hour Trading

Webull lets you buy US stocks and ETFs with as little as USD 1, useful if you want to start small or dollar-cost-average into expensive stocks without committing to a full share. It also supports trading outside standard US market hours, which matters for Malaysian investors given the time-zone gap with US regular trading sessions.

Dual Banking and Shariah Filtering

Webull supports both conventional and Islamic banking for deposits, and includes a Shariah-compliance indicator to filter stocks — a genuinely useful feature for investors who need to screen for Shariah-compliant holdings and don't want to cross-reference an external list manually.

Research Depth

Webull's charting and analysis tools (order book depth, analyst ratings, technical indicators) skew toward users who already know what they're looking at. It's a strength for anyone coming from a more advanced trading background, and a bit more of a learning curve for a first-time investor compared to Moomoo's more guided, AI-assisted approach.
