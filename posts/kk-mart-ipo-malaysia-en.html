How to apply for the KK Mart IPO

  1. Have a CDS account in your own name. Allotted shares go into a CDS account registered in your name, and only one application per person is accepted. Nominee accounts cannot be used: Rakuten Trade's own FAQ says its nominee account cannot apply for IPOs, and Webull also uses nominee accounts.
  2. Apply through a bank: use a participating bank's internet share application or ATM. The prospectus lists the participating banks.
  3. Or apply in the Moomoo app: open a CDS Account-IPO (Accounts, More, CDS Account-IPO), then apply in the IPO centre if KK Mart is listed there. Malaysian citizens only.
  4. Submit before 5pm on 2 December 2026, then check the ballot result on 4 December. Money for unsuccessful applications is refunded.

Where the IPO money goes

At 55 sen the IPO raises up to RM462 million, but only RM115.5 million (from the 210 million new shares) goes to KK Mart. Up to RM346.5 million comes from existing shares sold by K8 Resources and Arus Sejahtera. KK Mart's share is earmarked for distribution centre expansion (RM48.4 million, 41.9%), digital and IT (RM28 million, 24.2%), new stores (RM25 million, 21.7%), with the balance for bank loan repayment and listing expenses.

💡 About three quarters of the money raised is an offer for sale. That is not automatically a red flag, but most of the proceeds are not funding expansion.

How the business is doing

  • 996 stores as at 28 February 2026, mostly open 24 hours and concentrated in the Klang Valley.
  • FY2025 (year to 30 June 2025): revenue up 7.7% to RM1.57 billion, but net profit down 4.5% to RM96.98 million.
  • Q1 FY2027 revenue, released with the prospectus: up 7.1% to RM457.95 million.
  • 50.6% of sales happen between 10pm and 10am.
  • Plans: 240 new stores in FY2027 and more than 1,500 stores by June 2028.

Is the KK Mart IPO worth it? Valuation vs 99 Speed Mart

At 55 sen KK Mart is priced at about 19 times trailing earnings, against roughly 38 times for 99 Speed Mart (5326). That discount is the main argument for the IPO. The two are not like-for-like: 99 Speed Mart is far larger with a listed track record, while KK Mart's profit fell in its last full year. Whether the gap closes depends on profit keeping pace as KK Mart opens about 20 stores a month.

Risks to weigh: tobacco is about 25.5% of sales and alcohol about 12.9% (analysis cited by The Star), so tax or rule changes hit revenue directly, and third-party platforms show the stock as not Shariah-compliant (check the SC's official list). FY2025 profit fell while revenue grew. The 2024 socks controversy delayed the IPO and several stores were attacked; the company says it has tightened its SOPs and supply chain. New stores also take time to mature.

Didn't get any shares? Buying KK Mart after it lists on 15 December

If your ballot is unsuccessful, you can still buy KK Mart on the open market from listing day through any broker, including nominee-account brokers. KK Mart IPO FAQ

What is the KK Mart IPO price?

The retail price is 55 sen per share. The final retail price will be the lower of the institutional price (set by bookbuilding) and the 55 sen retail price.

When does the KK Mart IPO close?

Applications close at 5pm on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. Balloting is on 4 December and KK Mart lists on the Main Market on 15 December 2026.

How do I apply for the KK Mart IPO?

You need a CDS account in your own name. Apply through a participating bank's internet share application or ATM, or in the Moomoo app after opening a Moomoo CDS Account-IPO (Malaysian citizens only). Only one application per person is accepted.

Can I apply for the KK Mart IPO with Rakuten Trade or Webull?

Not with their standard accounts. Both hold shares in nominee accounts, and Rakuten Trade's FAQ says its nominee account cannot be used to apply for IPOs. You can use them to buy KK Mart after it lists on 15 December.

How long does it take to open a CDS account for the IPO?

Moomoo says a CDS Account-IPO application is usually processed in under 2 business days. Applications for KK Mart close on 2 December 2026, so open one as early as possible.

How many KK Mart IPO shares are for the public?

70 million shares are open to the Malaysian public by ballot, of which 35 million are reserved for Bumiputera applicants. Another 35 million are reserved for eligible persons through the Pink Form.

Is KK Mart cheaper than 99 Speed Mart?

At 55 sen KK Mart is priced at about 19 times trailing earnings, compared with roughly 38 times for 99 Speed Mart (5326), according to The Edge. 99 Speed Mart is much larger and has a listed track record.

Is KK Mart Shariah-compliant?

Third-party IPO platforms show KK Mart as not Shariah-compliant, and tobacco and alcohol make up a large share of sales. Check the Securities Commission's official Shariah list.
