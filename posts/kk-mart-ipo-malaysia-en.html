How to apply for the KK Mart IPO

  1. Have a CDS account in your own name. Allotted shares go into a CDS account registered in your name, and only one application per person is accepted. Nominee accounts cannot be used: Rakuten Trade's own FAQ says its nominee account cannot apply for IPOs, and Webull also uses nominee accounts.
  2. Apply through a bank: use a participating bank's internet share application or ATM. The prospectus lists the participating banks.
  3. Or apply in the Moomoo app: open a CDS Account-IPO (Accounts, More, CDS Account-IPO), then apply in the IPO centre if KK Mart is listed there. Malaysian citizens only.
  4. Submit before 5pm on 2 December 2026, then check the ballot result on 4 December. Money for unsuccessful applications is refunded.

Where the IPO money goes

At 55 sen the IPO raises up to RM462 million, but only RM115.5 million (from the 210 million new shares) goes to KK Mart. Up to RM346.5 million comes from existing shares sold by K8 Resources and Arus Sejahtera. KK Mart's share is earmarked for distribution centre expansion (RM48.4 million, 41.9%), digital and IT (RM28 million, 24.2%), new stores (RM25 million, 21.7%), with the balance for bank loan repayment and listing expenses.

💡 About three quarters of the money raised is an offer for sale. That is not automatically a red flag, but most of the proceeds are not funding expansion.

Things you must know before you apply for the KK Mart IPO

✅ Strengths

  1. SARA merchant. KK Mart accepts SARA aid spent through MyKad. Its FY2026 same-store sales growth of 1.7% was supported by SARA and Sarawak's SKAS aid.
  2. Open 24 hours. 50.6% of sales come between 10pm and 10am, most of it outside 99 Speed Mart's opening hours.
  3. Best net margin of the three. About 5.8%, against 5.4% for 99 Speed Mart and about 1.0% for 7-Eleven. Gross margin is about 29%, close to 7-Eleven's, but KK Mart controls costs far better.
  4. Owns its brand. No franchise fees, unlike a licensed brand.
  5. Cheapest valuation, with a dividend policy. About 18.8x PE, and a target payout of 40% to 60% of net profit, which works out to roughly 2.1% to 3.2% a year at 55 sen.

⚠️ Weaknesses

  1. Not Shariah-compliant. Tobacco and alcohol sales rule it out, so Islamic funds and EPF's Simpanan Shariah cannot buy it, which shrinks the pool of institutional buyers. 99 Speed Mart and 7-Eleven are not Shariah-compliant either, and 99 Speed Mart still trades at about 38x.
  2. Past controversies point to weak product controls. The 2024 socks controversy led to petrol-bomb attacks on stores and delayed the IPO, and early 2025 brought a sandwich mislabelling issue. Both trace back to supplier and product vetting. The company says it has since tightened its SOPs and supply chain.
  3. Recent same-store sales are negative. The prospectus discloses same-store sales of minus 6% from July to October 2026, partly from disruption after a new warehouse management system went live.
  4. Heavy reliance on tobacco at about a quarter of sales.
  5. Most of the money goes to sellers. 75% of the RM462 million raised goes to the founders' K8 Resources and private equity investor Dymon Asia.

Key catalysts

  • Bigger SARA payouts in 2027. Budget 2027 raises SARA for STR recipients to up to RM150 a month (from RM100), and gives RM100 twice to Malaysians aged 18 and above who are not on STR: before Hari Raya Aidilfitri in March and for National Day in August 2027. This is spending that has to happen at participating stores.
  • Aggressive expansion. About 20 new stores a month in FY2027, rising to 24 a month in FY2028, for more than 1,500 stores by June 2028, including first entries into Terengganu, Perlis, Kelantan and Sabah.
  • Valuation gap. At about 18.8x against 38x for 99 Speed Mart, part of the gap could close if KK Mart shows steady profit growth after listing.
  • Warehouse fix. If the warehouse system disruption is resolved, same-store sales should recover from the minus 6% seen in July to October.

Key threats

  • Staff costs. The minimum wage rises 17.6% from RM1,700 to RM2,000 in June 2027 for companies with annual sales above RM50 million, which includes KK Mart. Round-the-clock stores need more shifts, so this hits a 24-hour chain harder. 7-Eleven's profit dropped after the February 2025 wage increase, which shows how sensitive this business is to it.
  • Weak store productivity. Same-store sales fell 6% in July to October 2026 while the chain keeps adding stores, and new stores take time to mature.
  • Tobacco rules and taxes. With tobacco at about 25.5% of sales, higher excise duty or tighter rules would hit revenue directly.
  • Brand risk. Another product slip could trigger a boycott, as in 2024.
  • A stronger US dollar: an indirect risk. KK Mart sells mainly local brands bought in ringgit, and its house-brand products come through 27 local suppliers, some manufactured overseas. We did not find large direct US dollar purchases in the public information. A stronger dollar would mainly come through suppliers raising prices on imported goods and ingredients, which KK Mart would need to pass on.

KK Mart IPO FAQ

What is the KK Mart IPO price?

The retail price is 55 sen per share. The final retail price will be the lower of the institutional price (set by bookbuilding) and the 55 sen retail price.

When does the KK Mart IPO close?

Applications close at 5pm on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. Balloting is on 4 December and KK Mart lists on the Main Market on 15 December 2026.

How do I apply for the KK Mart IPO?

You need a CDS account in your own name. Apply through a participating bank's internet share application or ATM, or in the Moomoo app after opening a Moomoo CDS Account-IPO (Malaysian citizens only). Only one application per person is accepted.

Can I apply for the KK Mart IPO with Rakuten Trade or Webull?

Not with their standard accounts. Both hold shares in nominee accounts, and Rakuten Trade's FAQ says its nominee account cannot be used to apply for IPOs. You can use them to buy KK Mart after it lists on 15 December.

How long does it take to open a CDS account for the IPO?

Moomoo says a CDS Account-IPO application is usually processed in under 2 business days. Applications for KK Mart close on 2 December 2026, so open one as early as possible.

How many KK Mart IPO shares are for the public?

70 million shares are open to the Malaysian public by ballot, of which 35 million are reserved for Bumiputera applicants. Another 35 million are reserved for eligible persons through the Pink Form.

Is KK Mart cheaper than 99 Speed Mart?

Yes on PE. At 55 sen KK Mart is priced at about 18.8 times FY2026 earnings, against roughly 38 times for 99 Speed Mart (5326) and above 70 times for 7-Eleven Malaysia (5250). KK Mart also has the best net margin of the three at about 5.8%, but it is much smaller and its same-store sales fell 6% in July to October 2026.

What are KK Mart's latest results?

For the year to 30 June 2026, KK Mart made a net profit of RM102.5 million on revenue of RM1.77 billion, up from RM97 million and RM1.57 billion a year earlier. It had 1,106 stores as at 31 October 2026.

Is KK Mart Shariah-compliant?

No. KK Mart sells tobacco and alcohol, and third-party platforms show it as not Shariah-compliant, so Islamic funds cannot buy it. 99 Speed Mart and 7-Eleven Malaysia are not Shariah-compliant either. Check the Securities Commission's official list for the final status.

Does KK Mart accept SARA?

Yes. KK Mart is a SARA merchant, so recipients can spend their SARA aid there with MyKad. Budget 2027 raises SARA to up to RM150 a month for STR recipients and gives other adults RM100 twice in 2027.
