What KK Mart does, and where the IPO money goes

KK Mart runs 1,106 stores (as at 31 October 2026), most of them open 24 hours, and more than half of its sales (50.6%) happen between 10pm and 10am. In the year to June 2026 it made a net profit of RM102.5 million on revenue of RM1.77 billion.

One thing to know: 75% of the IPO shares (630 million) are existing shares sold by the current owners, mainly the founder, so that money goes to them. Only 25% (210 million new shares) raises money for the company itself: RM115.5 million for distribution centres, new stores and IT.

The one-line takeaway: KK Mart earns 7-Eleven-like gross margins but keeps far more of it as profit, and it is priced at half of 99 Speed Mart's PE. The trade-off is a smaller, younger network and a weak run of same-store sales.

Things you must know before you apply

Strengths

  • SARA merchant: government aid is spent at its stores
  • Open 24 hours: half its sales come at night
  • Best net margin of the three listed chains (about 5.8%)
  • Owns its brand, so no franchise fees
  • Cheapest PE (about 18.8x) with a 40% to 60% dividend payout policy

Weaknesses

  • Not Shariah-compliant, so Islamic funds cannot buy it (same for 99 Speed Mart and 7-Eleven)
  • The 2024 socks controversy showed gaps in product checks
  • Same-store sales fell 6% in July to October 2026 after a warehouse system problem
  • Tobacco is about a quarter of sales
  • 75% of the IPO shares are the founder's existing shares

What could sway your decision

Beyond the basics, these are the things that could swing whether KK Mart is worth applying for.

Catalysts

  • Bigger SARA in 2027: up to RM150 a month for STR recipients, plus RM100 twice for other adults.
  • Fast expansion: 20 to 24 new stores a month, for more than 1,500 stores by June 2028.
  • Room to re-rate: about half of 99 Speed Mart's PE.
  • Warehouse fix: same-store sales could recover once the system issue is resolved.

Threats

  • Higher wages: the minimum wage rises to RM2,000 (+17.6%) in June 2027, and 24-hour stores need more shifts.
  • Weaker store sales while the chain keeps opening new stores.
  • Tobacco tax or rule changes hit a quarter of sales.
  • Another product slip could bring back boycotts.
  • A stronger US dollar raises the cost of imported goods (an indirect risk).

Now you can decide

If you like the cheap valuation, the SARA tailwind and the 24-hour model, KK Mart may be worth a ballot. If the wage increase, the recent drop in same-store sales or the brand risk bother you, it is fine to sit this one out and watch a few quarters of results after listing. It is your call. How to apply for the KK Mart IPO, step by step

  1. Get a CDS account in your own name. If you use Moomoo: Accounts, More, CDS Account-IPO. Do it now, because approval takes up to 2 business days and applications close on 2 December.
  2. Apply in the Moomoo IPO centre (Malaysian citizens), or through a participating bank's internet share application or ATM.
  3. Submit before 5pm on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. One application per person only.
  4. Check the ballot result on 4 December. If you are unsuccessful, your money is refunded.
  5. Listing day is 15 December. Allotted shares are in your CDS account before then.

KK Mart IPO FAQ

What is the KK Mart IPO price?

The retail price is 55 sen per share. The final retail price will be the lower of the institutional price (set by bookbuilding) and the 55 sen retail price.

When does the KK Mart IPO close?

Applications close at 5pm on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. Balloting is on 4 December and KK Mart lists on the Main Market on 15 December 2026.

How do I apply for the KK Mart IPO?

You need a CDS account in your own name. Apply through a participating bank's internet share application or ATM, or in the Moomoo app after opening a Moomoo CDS Account-IPO (Malaysian citizens only). Only one application per person is accepted.

Can I apply for the KK Mart IPO with Rakuten Trade or Webull?

Not with their standard accounts. Both hold shares in nominee accounts, and Rakuten Trade's FAQ says its nominee account cannot be used to apply for IPOs. You can use them to buy KK Mart after it lists on 15 December.

How long does it take to open a CDS account for the IPO?

Moomoo says a CDS Account-IPO application is usually processed in under 2 business days. Applications for KK Mart close on 2 December 2026, so open one as early as possible.

How many KK Mart IPO shares are for the public?

70 million shares are open to the Malaysian public by ballot, of which 35 million are reserved for Bumiputera applicants. Another 35 million are reserved for eligible persons through the Pink Form.

Is KK Mart cheaper than 99 Speed Mart?

Yes on PE. At 55 sen KK Mart is priced at about 18.8 times FY2026 earnings, against roughly 38 times for 99 Speed Mart (5326) and above 70 times for 7-Eleven Malaysia (5250). KK Mart also has the best net margin of the three at about 5.8%, but it is much smaller and its same-store sales fell 6% in July to October 2026.

What are KK Mart's latest results?

For the year to 30 June 2026, KK Mart made a net profit of RM102.5 million on revenue of RM1.77 billion, up from RM97 million and RM1.57 billion a year earlier. It had 1,106 stores as at 31 October 2026.

Is KK Mart Shariah-compliant?

No. KK Mart sells tobacco and alcohol, and third-party platforms show it as not Shariah-compliant, so Islamic funds cannot buy it. 99 Speed Mart and 7-Eleven Malaysia are not Shariah-compliant either. Check the Securities Commission's official list for the final status.

Does KK Mart accept SARA?

Yes. KK Mart is a SARA merchant, so recipients can spend their SARA aid there with MyKad. Budget 2027 raises SARA to up to RM150 a month for STR recipients and gives other adults RM100 twice in 2027.
