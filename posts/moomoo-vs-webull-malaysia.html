IPO and CDS: Which Is Easier?

You can buy and sell Bursa shares on both apps without opening anything extra. Bursa IPOs are where they differ. Moomoo asks you to open a separate CDS Account-IPO inside the app (Accounts, More, CDS Account-IPO), which Moomoo says usually takes under 2 business days. Webull opens a nominee account at sign-up, so check with Webull before an IPO you care about. If IPOs or an employer Pink Form are part of your plan, read our CDS account guide first.

Is Moomoo or Webull Safer?

They are equally safe on the points that matter. Both Moomoo Securities Malaysia Sdn. Bhd. and Webull Securities (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd are licensed by the Securities Commission Malaysia, and client assets with both are covered by the Capital Market Compensation Fund up to RM100,000 per investor if the broker itself fails. Neither protects you from your investments falling in value.

Which Persona Fits Which Broker?

Already comfortable reading charts and want to trade US stocks heavily. The 365-day 0% US commission offer for new users and extended trading hours are genuinely strong for this use case, and you're comfortable with the RM5,000 deposit for the reward.

The Honest Answer: Why Not Both?

There's no rule against holding accounts with multiple SC-licensed brokers, and plenty of investors do exactly that: Moomoo for Bursa Malaysia exposure and the AI research tools, Webull specifically for US trades to use its 0% US commission offer. If you're depositing enough to qualify for both welcome rewards, splitting your capital this way isn't unreasonable. If you're starting with a smaller amount, pick the one that matches where you'll actually trade most, and don't let the size of the sign-up bonus be the deciding factor on its own.

What We'd Actually Do

If we were opening our first brokerage account today with no strong market preference either way, we'd lean Moomoo for the lower entry deposit and the AI research assistant, because it shortens the learning curve. If US stocks are the main focus from day one, Webull's 365-day US commission offer is hard to argue with on pure cost grounds.

Bottom Line

Both are SC-regulated, both have real (not misleading) welcome rewards, and neither is a bad choice. Moomoo wins on accessibility and research tools for beginners; Webull wins on US-market economics and depth for more active traders. Read our full standalone reviews for the details behind each.
