Which Persona Fits Which Broker?

Choose Moomoo if you're...

A first-time investor who wants Bursa Malaysia and global markets in one app, with AI-assisted research to lean on while you learn, and you can hit the RM1,000+ deposit tier to unlock a bigger reward with code BURSA88.

Choose Webull if you're...

Already comfortable reading charts and want to trade US stocks heavily — the 0% commission through end-2026 and 24-hour trading window are genuinely strong for this use case, and you're comfortable with the RM5,000 deposit for the reward.

The Honest Answer: Why Not Both?

There's no rule against holding accounts with multiple SC-licensed brokers, and plenty of investors do exactly that — Moomoo for Bursa Malaysia exposure and the AI research tools, Webull specifically for US trades to capture the 0% commission window. If you're depositing enough to qualify for both welcome rewards, splitting your capital this way isn't unreasonable. If you're starting with a smaller amount, pick the one that matches where you'll actually trade most, and don't let the size of the sign-up bonus be the deciding factor on its own.

What We'd Actually Do

If we were opening our first brokerage account today with no strong market preference either way, we'd lean Moomoo for the lower entry deposit and the AI research assistant — it shortens the learning curve. If US stocks are the main focus from day one, Webull's fee structure through 2026 is hard to argue with on pure cost grounds.

Bottom Line

Both are SC-regulated, both have real (not misleading) welcome rewards, and neither is a bad choice. Moomoo wins on accessibility and research tools for beginners; Webull wins on US-market economics and depth for more active traders. Read our full standalone reviews for the details behind each.
