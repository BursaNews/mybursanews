Am I eligible for the KK Mart Pink Form?

Not every KK Mart worker gets one. The prospectus defines Eligible Persons as KK Mart's directors, employees of the group who meet the board's criteria, and persons who have contributed to the group's success. If you are eligible, you receive a Pink Application Form with a letter stating how many shares are reserved for you and how to apply. No letter usually means you are not in the allocation. Ask your HR department if you are unsure.

Not on the list? You can still apply under the public ballot like any Malaysian. See how to apply for the KK Mart IPO.

No CDS account in your own name? How to get one before 2 December

  4. Write that CDS account number on your Pink Application Form, then follow your letter's instructions to pay and submit.

Common Pink Form mistakes that get applications rejected

  • Using a nominee account instead of a CDS account in your own name.
  • Name mismatch between the form, your MyKad and your CDS account.
  • Missing the closing time. Late applications are generally not accepted.
  • Paying the wrong amount or not following the payment method in your letter.

After allotment: selling or holding your KK Mart shares

Allotted shares are credited to your CDS account before listing on 15 December 2026. To sell, you need a trading account at the broker where that CDS account sits. If you used Moomoo's CDS Account-IPO, Moomoo says allotted IPO shares are moved to your universal account before listing so you can trade them there. Check the prospectus and your letter for any restriction that applies to you, for example as a director.

KK Mart Pink Form FAQ

Who can apply for the KK Mart IPO Pink Form?

Only Eligible Persons invited by KK Mart: its directors, employees who meet the board's criteria, and persons who have contributed to the group. They receive a Pink Application Form and a letter stating their allocation.

How many shares are in the KK Mart Pink Form allocation?

35 million new shares at the 55 sen retail price are reserved for Eligible Persons under the Pink Form allocation.

Can I use a nominee account for the KK Mart Pink Form?

No. The application needs a CDS account in your own name. Nominee accounts, such as standard Rakuten Trade or Webull accounts, cannot be used for IPO applications.

Can I use Moomoo for the KK Mart Pink Form?

Moomoo's CDS Account-IPO is a direct CDS account in your own name, so you can put its CDS number on your Pink Application Form. Submit the form itself as instructed in your letter, and confirm with HR or the issuing house if unsure.

When is the KK Mart Pink Form deadline?

IPO applications close at 5pm on Wednesday, 2 December 2026. Follow the exact closing time printed in your Pink Form letter.

I work at KK Mart but did not get a Pink Form. Can I still apply?

Yes, under the public ballot like any Malaysian, through a participating bank or the Moomoo app with a CDS account in your own name.

When will I get my KK Mart shares?

Allotted shares are credited to your CDS account before KK Mart lists on the Main Market on 15 December 2026.
