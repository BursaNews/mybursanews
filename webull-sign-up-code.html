  2. 2
    Download the Webull App & Create Account
    Download the Webull app from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Register using your email address and Malaysian mobile number, then set a secure password.
  3. 3
    Complete eKYC Verification
    Upload your Malaysian NRIC (front and back) for digital identity verification. The process is fully online — no branch visits or physical documents required. Typically approved within a few hours.
  4. 4
    Your CDS Account is Opened Automatically
    Webull automatically opens a CDS (Central Depository System) account for you during the registration flow. This is required to hold Bursa Malaysia stocks. No separate application is needed.

    Deposit RM5,000 via Bank Transfer
    Fund your account via bank transfer. Go to Deposit → Bank Transfer in the app and follow the instructions. Make sure the depositor name matches your Webull account name. Funds typically arrive within minutes to a few hours.
  6. 6
    Start Trading with 0% Commission
    You're ready. Trade US stocks and ETFs on NYSE and NASDAQ with 0% commission for 365 days from your account activation date. Your RM300 trading vouchers will appear in the Rewards Centre in the Webull app shortly after account approval.
