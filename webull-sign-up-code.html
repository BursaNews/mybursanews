How to Sign Up for Webull Malaysia

Follow these steps to claim all available rewards.

  1. 1
    Tap the BursaNews Referral Link
    Start by tapping the sign-up button on this page or the link above. Signing up through our link is how Webull records that you were referred by BursaNews. Webull's RM300 new-user reward is its standard campaign; check its terms in the app.
  2. 2
    Download the Webull App & Create Account
    Download the Webull app from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Register using your email address and Malaysian mobile number, then set a secure password.
  3. 3
    Complete eKYC Verification
    Upload your Malaysian NRIC (front and back) for digital identity verification. The process is fully online — no branch visits or physical documents required. Typically approved within a few hours.
  4. 4
    Your Nominees Account will be Opened
    Webull automatically opens a Nominee account for you during the registration flow. No separate application is needed.
    ℹ️ In a nominee structure your Bursa shares are held by the broker's nominee on your behalf. If you need a CDS account in your own name (for example for an IPO Pink Form), ask Webull first. To qualify for the RM300 reward, keep a net deposit of RM5,000 for 60 days; the reward is credited after the 60-day period, not immediately.
  5. 5
    Deposit RM5,000 via Bank Transfer
    Fund your account via bank transfer. Go to Deposit → Bank Transfer in the app and follow the instructions. Make sure the depositor name matches your Webull account name. Funds typically arrive within minutes to a few hours.
  6. 6
    Start Trading
    You're ready. New users get 0 commission on US stocks for 365 days under Webull's current promotion. Once you have held the qualifying deposit for 60 days, your RM300 reward appears in the Rewards Centre in the Webull app.
