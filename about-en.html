Why We Started BursaNews

BursaNews (马股报报看) is founded by two Malaysians with 10+ years each in Malaysia's capital markets, investment banking, audit, and valuation.

During our years in capital markets, we noticed the same pattern over and over: most retail investors were making decisions based on tips, rumours, and half-understood news, not because they lacked intelligence, but because nobody had ever shown them how to read the real signals.

We know how to read financial figures inside-out, because it was literally our job. We understand the logic and hidden meaning behind every Bursa announcement, every corporate exercise, every set of quarterly results. And we thought: why not share that, simply, honestly, with a bit of humour?

Our Mission

To minimise the gap in financial information interpretation, and educate everyday Malaysians to see the market the way professionals do. Not by giving them stock tips. By giving them the knowledge to think for themselves.

(Also, because financial content is way too boring and we wanted to fix that too 😂)

Why Our Analysis Hits Different

Most finance content creators you see online hold an MR (Markets Representative) licence, the basic licence required to talk about investments publicly. We held that too. But we went a step further.

What does this mean for you? Preparing and reading Bursa announcements was literally our day job. When a listed company makes a move, a rights issue, a private placement, an acquisition, we understand the “why” behind it. Not just what the numbers say on the surface, but what the company is trying to achieve, and what's happening behind the curtain that most people on the street never get to see.

We're not trying to brag about qualifications, plenty of qualified people produce terrible financial content. What we’re saying is: the insights in every BursaNews report come from people who have sat in those boardrooms, written those announcements, and advised on those transactions.
