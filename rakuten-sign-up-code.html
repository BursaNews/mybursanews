How Rakuten Trade Is Regulated

Rakuten Trade Sdn Bhd is a joint venture between Rakuten Securities Inc. (a subsidiary of Japan's Rakuten Group) and Kenanga Investment Bank Berhad. It is regulated by the Securities Commission Malaysia (SC) under a Capital Markets Services Licence.

Client funds are held separately from Rakuten Trade's own finances through Kenanga Investment Bank as custodian, and your CDS (Central Depository System) holdings for Bursa Malaysia stocks are protected under Bursa Malaysia's own depository framework — the same protection that applies across all Bursa-regulated brokers.
