06 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this investment advice? Will you tell me what to buy or sell?

No. 马股早知道 is analysis and interpretation of publicly available information: what the numbers mean, what management said, and what the risks and catalysts are. We don't give buy or sell tips. Your investment decisions stay yours.

How many analyses will I get each day?

Around 2 to 6 per trading day, depending on how many important announcements came out the day before. Expect more during results season.

What language are the reports in?

Mainly Chinese, keeping common English financial terms such as orderbook, PE and margin, so you can match them to company announcements.

How do I pay, and when do I start receiving analysis?

WhatsApp us, then pay by bank transfer, TNG or DuitNow. Once payment is confirmed we activate you manually and add you to the WhatsApp group. You should start receiving analysis from the next trading day.

Is there auto-renewal or auto-debit?

No. There's no auto-renewal and no recurring deduction. When your plan ends, you renew only if it helped you.
