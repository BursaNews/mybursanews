How to Sign Up for Moomoo Malaysia

Complete these steps to unlock the standard welcome reward and the BURSA88 partner bonus.

  1. 1
    Download the Moomoo App
    Download the official Moomoo app from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Search "Moomoo: Invest & Trade Stocks".
  2. 2
    Create Your Account
    Tap Sign Up, enter your email address and set a secure password. Select Malaysia as your country of residence.
  3. 3
    Complete eKYC Verification
    Upload your Malaysian NRIC (front and back). Moomoo verifies your identity digitally — usually approved within minutes during business hours. No physical documents needed.
  4. 4
    Know How Bursa Holdings and IPOs Work
    You can trade Bursa Malaysia stocks from your Moomoo universal account. If you want to apply for Malaysian IPOs (including a staff IPO Pink Form), Moomoo's help centre says you need a separate CDS Account-IPO, opened in the app under Accounts › More › CDS Account-IPO. New to this? Read what a CDS account is.
  5. 5
    Deposit RM1,000+ and Enter Code BURSA88
    Fund your account from a bank account in your own name (for example via FPX online banking). The minimum qualifying deposit is RM1,000, and all deposits count only if made within 30 days of your first deposit. At the referral or promo code field, enter BURSA88 for the BursaNews partner bonus.
  6. 6
    Tap "Claim" on the Campaign Page
    After depositing, return to the welcome campaign page (in the app: Me → Promotions → New User Rewards) and tap "Claim" within the campaign period.
    ⚠️ This step is mandatory. Many users miss it and do not receive their rewards even after depositing the qualifying amount. Do not skip this step.
  7. 7
    Hold Your Funds for 60 Days
    Keep your net deposit at or above your tier for 60 days. You can trade freely, and small price movements do not affect eligibility, but Moomoo notes that if your holdings lose more than 20% in value the unlocking task may fail (contact Moomoo support if that happens). Apple share rewards are credited within 10 trading days after unlocking.

Do I need to tap "Claim" after depositing?

Yes. Many users miss this. After funding your account, return to the welcome campaign page in the Moomoo app and tap "Claim" within the campaign period. Without this step, your rewards may not be activated even if you meet the deposit threshold.

Can I trade during the 60-day holding period?

Yes. You can trade Malaysian and US stocks freely during the 60-day period. Your net deposit needs to stay at or above your tier. Small price movements are fine, but Moomoo notes a fall of more than 20% in holdings value may cause the unlocking task to fail.

Yes. Moomoo Malaysia is operated by Moomoo Securities Malaysia Sdn. Bhd., licensed by the Securities Commission (SC) Malaysia. Client assets are also protected by the Capital Market Compensation Fund up to RM100,000. Read our full Moomoo Malaysia review for a deeper breakdown.

Does Moomoo open a CDS account for me?

You can trade Bursa stocks from your Moomoo universal account. For Malaysian IPO subscriptions, Moomoo's help centre says you need a separate CDS Account-IPO, opened in the app (Accounts › More › CDS Account-IPO) and usually processed in under 2 business days. See our CDS account guide.
