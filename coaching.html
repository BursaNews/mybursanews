Not necessarily. If you already know how to buy stocks and have basic investing knowledge, you can join Course 2 directly. Not sure? WhatsApp us and we'll advise you.

Our primary language is Mandarin Chinese, with English terms used where relevant. If your group prefers English, let us know when you register.

We can’t promise returns, and you should be cautious of anyone who does. What we teach is how to analyse companies and make more informed decisions. Investing always involves risk.
