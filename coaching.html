Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the courses suitable for?

Course 1 is for complete beginners with no finance background. Course 2 is for those who already understand the basics and want to go deeper into reading financial reports.

Why minimum 2 persons?

We keep classes very small so every student gets direct attention. Minimum 2 ensures the class runs. You're welcome to bring a friend, spouse, sibling or colleague!

Online or in-person?

We can accommodate both online (Zoom/Google Meet) and in-person depending on your group's preference. WhatsApp us to confirm.

Will materials be provided?

Yes. Each session includes a summary slide deck and key takeaways. We also provide a list of free resources for continued learning.

Do I need Course 1 before Course 2?

Not necessarily. If you already know how to buy stocks and have basic investing knowledge, you can join Course 2 directly. Not sure? WhatsApp us and we'll advise you.

What language are the sessions in?

Our primary language is Mandarin Chinese, with English terms used where relevant. If your group prefers English, let us know when you register.
