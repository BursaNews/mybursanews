Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the courses suitable for?

Course 1 is for complete beginners — you don't need any finance or accounting background. Course 2 is for those who already understand the basics of buying stocks and want to go deeper into reading financial reports.

Why do you need minimum 2 persons?

We keep classes very small so every student gets direct attention. The minimum of 2 persons ensures the class actually runs and makes it worthwhile for everyone involved. You're welcome to bring a friend, spouse, sibling or colleague!

Is the class conducted online or in person?

Please WhatsApp us to confirm the format. We can accommodate both online (via Zoom/Google Meet) and in-person sessions depending on the group's preference and location.

Will there be any materials provided?

Yes. Each session comes with a summary slide deck and key takeaways you can refer to after the class. We also provide a list of free resources for you to continue learning on your own.

Do I need to complete Course 1 before taking Course 2?

Not necessarily. If you already understand how to buy stocks and have basic investing knowledge, you can join Course 2 directly. If you're unsure, just WhatsApp us and we'll help you decide which course is right for you.

Are the sessions in Mandarin or English?

Our primary language is Mandarin Chinese, with English terms used where relevant. If your group prefers English, please let us know when you register and we will accommodate.
