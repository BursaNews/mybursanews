Short answer

At standard rates, there is no single cheapest broker. Rakuten Trade is cheapest for trades up to RM100 and again from about RM5,760 to RM9,999. Webull is cheapest from RM100 to about RM5,760 and from RM10,000 to RM15,000. Above RM15,000, Moomoo is cheapest. What the numbers actually mean

  • Minimum fees hurt small trades the most. A broker charging 0.10% with a RM8 minimum takes RM8 on a RM1,000 purchase. That is 0.8% of your money before the share price moves at all, and 1.6% once you sell. On the same trade, Webull charges RM2.50 and Rakuten Trade RM2.88.
  • Percentage fees hurt big trades the most. On a RM50,000 order, Rakuten Trade's 0.1% tier costs RM50, Webull RM25 and Moomoo RM18 at its standard rate.
  • Stamp duty is often your biggest cost, and you cannot avoid it. At RM1 per RM1,000, stamp duty on a RM10,000 trade is RM10, more than the brokerage at any of the three brokers. Do not switch brokers to save RM0.38 if the real cost is elsewhere.
  • Fees are a tie-breaker, not the whole decision. CDS arrangement (own-name or nominee, which matters for IPOs), US market access, app quality and support count too. Our full broker comparison covers those.

Brokerage fee FAQ

Which broker has the cheapest brokerage fee in Malaysia?

It depends on your trade size. At standard rates, Rakuten Trade is cheapest up to RM100 and from about RM5,760 to RM9,999, Webull from RM100 to about RM5,760 and from RM10,000 to RM15,000, and Moomoo above RM15,000. New Moomoo users pay only a RM3 platform fee for 180 days.

What is Moomoo's brokerage fee in Malaysia?

New users pay 0% commission on Bursa trades for 180 days, then 0.03% of trade value. A RM3 platform fee applies to every Bursa order from day one, including during the free-commission period.

What is Rakuten Trade's brokerage fee?

RM1 for trades up to RM100, RM2.88 from RM100.01 to RM9,999.99, 0.1% from RM10,000 to RM99,999.99, and RM100 flat above RM100,000.

What is Webull's brokerage fee in Malaysia?

0.05% of the trade amount with a minimum of RM2.50 per trade, and no platform fee.

Are the clearing fee and stamp duty the same at every broker?

Yes. The 0.03% clearing fee (capped at RM1,000) and stamp duty of RM1 per RM1,000 (capped at RM1,000) are passed through by every broker, so they never change which broker is cheapest.

Is SST charged on Bursa brokerage?

Brokerage on ordinary shares is exempt. Since 1 October 2025, 8% SST applies to brokerage on ETFs, REITs, rights and warrants, and to some service fees such as platform fees. Check your contract note for the exact amount.
