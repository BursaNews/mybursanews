Is Webull Malaysia regulated and safe?

Webull Securities (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd holds a Capital Markets Services Licence (CMSL) from the Securities Commission Malaysia, the same regulatory framework that governs every legal Malaysian stockbroker.

Client funds are kept separate from Webull's own finances through a custodian bank arrangement, consistent with SC requirements for licensed brokers. Globally, Webull is also licensed across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, South Africa, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Japan — the Malaysia entity operates under its own local licence rather than relying on any foreign registration.

Worth noting: unlicensed "Webull" access via foreign platforms (not the SC-regulated Malaysia entity) carries real regulatory risk for Malaysian residents. Always confirm you're using Webull Securities (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd specifically.
