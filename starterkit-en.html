Mission 2: Learn 5 Words Only
Forget everything else. Just these 5. That's enough.
✓
▼

If you remember nothing else from investing school, just know these 5 words. You'll sound smarter than 90% of people at the next family dinner.

Profit
Money left after all costs. The real number that matters.
Dividend
Cash paid to you just for holding the stock. Like rental income.
PE Ratio
A PE of 10 simply means the company needs to earn 10 years of profit to equal what you paid for it. Lower PE = you get your money back faster. Higher PE = it takes longer.
Revenue
Total sales money coming in, before any costs.
Portfolio
All your stocks combined. Your total investment collection.
  • ✓
    I know what Profit and Revenue mean

    Revenue = money in. Profit = money in minus costs. Simple.

  • ✓
    I understand Dividend and PE Ratio

    Dividend = cash bonus for holding. PE = how many years of profit it takes to pay back your investment.

  • ✓
    I've memorised all 5 words

    Test yourself: Profit, Dividend, PE Ratio, Revenue, Portfolio. Say them out loud.
